Shellbark Lodge

Founded in the 1930s, Shellbark Lodge has long been a center of fellowship, leadership, and service. With 22 active members, we carry on a proud tradition of brotherhood, outdoor adventure, and commitment to the land and community. Beyond fellowship, our traditions include hunting, fishing, and other outdoor adventures throughout the rural Pennsylvania woods.
