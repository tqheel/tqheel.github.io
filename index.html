Shellbark Lodge

Founded in the 1930s, Shellbark Lodge has long been a center of fellowship, leadership, and service. With 22 active members, we carry on a proud tradition of brotherhood, outdoor adventure, and dedication to our community. Beyond fellowship, we are rooted in the traditions of hunting, fishing, and other outdoor adventures in the rural Pennsylvania woods.
